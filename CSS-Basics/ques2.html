Welcome to Paris

Paris is one of the most beautiful and famous cities in the world. It is known for its art, culture, architecture and romantic atmosphere.

About Paris

Paris is the capital city of France. It is located along the River Seine and is famous for its historical buildings, museums and beautiful streets.

The city attracts millions of visitors every year because of its rich culture and unique architecture.

Famous Places

Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most recognizable landmarks in the world.

Louvre Museum

The Louvre is one of the world's largest and most famous museums.

Notre-Dame

Notre-Dame is a historic Gothic cathedral located in the heart of Paris.

Famous French Food

Paris is also famous for delicious French cuisine. Some popular foods include croissants, baguettes, macarons and French pastries.
