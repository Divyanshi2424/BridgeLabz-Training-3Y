The night had finally begun to fade, leaving behind a sky painted in Soft shades of gold and blue. Mira stood beside the old window, watching the first rays of sunlight touch the sleeping town.For the first time in years, she felt that something beautiful was waiting for her.

She picked up the small letter lying on the table. Three words were written on it: “Begin Again.” Her heart filled with hope, and a quiet smile appeared on her face.Perhaps every ending was simply another new beginning waiting to be discovered.

Outside, the birds began to sing, the flowers opened their petals, and the world slowly awakened. Mira took a deep breath and stepped toward the door, carrying nothing but courage, hope, and a new dream.

Novel:The Whisper of Dawn
